Medical Certificates

Request a medical certificate for an absence from work or study following an appropriate GP assessment.

Providing requested dates does not guarantee that a certificate will be issued or that those dates will be approved.

How medical certificates work

  1. Choose whether you need a certificate for work or study.
  2. Enter the dates you would like the clinician to consider.
  3. Identify a related completed consultation, or arrange one if needed.
  4. Review the request before submitting it for GP assessment.
  5. If approved and issued, the document may be made available through your patient account.

Important: Only a clinician can decide whether a medical certificate is appropriate and what period it covers.

Important information

  • Medical certificates are not automatically generated when a form is submitted.
  • The GP may need a consultation or additional information to make an assessment.
  • Dates entered here are a request, not certified dates.
  • Any fee must be explained before a real payment or submission is confirmed.
  • This V1 demonstration creates no certificate and stores no medical details.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a certificate for university?

Yes. The proposed request flow includes study absence, subject to GP assessment.

Can I request dates in the past?

You may explain your requested period to the clinician, but only the clinician can decide whether certification is appropriate.

Will I receive a certificate immediately?

No. The request must be assessed, and issuing a certificate is not guaranteed.

What if I have not had a consultation?

The proposed website should direct you to book a GP consultation before continuing, where required.
