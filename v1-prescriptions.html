Prescriptions

Manage your repeat prescription requests online without needing to phone the clinic for every routine enquiry.

This service is for patients requesting a repeat of a medicine already recorded in their clinic profile. Every request needs assessment by a GP.

How prescriptions work

  1. Check that your medicine is already in your patient record.
  2. Select the medicine and your preferred collection pharmacy.
  3. Review your request and submit it for GP assessment.
  4. Check the request status in your patient account. An approved electronic prescription may be sent to the selected pharmacy.

Important: A submitted request is not an approved prescription. A clinician may need to speak with you or arrange an appointment.

Important information

  • Repeat prescriptions are issued only when the GP considers them clinically appropriate.
  • If your medicine, symptoms or dose has changed, a consultation may be required.
  • Any service fee should be clearly displayed before a real payment or submission is confirmed.
  • Information may need to be shared securely with your selected pharmacy for prescription fulfilment.
  • This V1 demonstration stores no request and makes no payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request a new medicine here?

No. This form illustrates requests for medicines already in a patient's record. New medicines require clinical assessment.

Will my medicine be ready immediately?

Not necessarily. Submission starts a review; the GP decides whether to issue a prescription.

Can I choose the pharmacy?

Yes. The proposed service allows patients to select a preferred pharmacy before submitting the request.

What if I need help?

Use the clinic's contact or appointment service in the full group website. In an emergency, call 111.
